Auto climate

Follow the room temperature
Room reading from the DHT11 on the main board. One fan level more above 70 % humidity. Changing heat or fan by hand turns auto climate off.

Puck light

#FF6A00

Effect

The ring always follows your finger on the puck. Faults blink red and low battery pulses orange at 6 o'clock, whatever the effect.

Battery and charging

Charging
Battery care (charge to 80 %, 16.0 V)
Thermistor taped to the pack (J9)
Without a pack thermistor the charger ignores battery temperature. Heat and fan stop below 12.8 V on battery.

Heat levels

The board cuts power on its own at about 55 °C, and the firmware stops at 52 °C.
